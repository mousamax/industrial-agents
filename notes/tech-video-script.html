Before recording

  • Narrow the door again. O149 in data/MUCL/building.json is back to a 2.0 m opening. Scene 1 needs it as a 0.6 m door ("kind": "door", "width_m": 0.6), committed so the app loads it.
  • Say “entrance”, not “reception”. The plan has no room called reception, and Navi says so on camera.
  • Say the 75 cm. Navi doesn't assume a wheelchair width; once said, it remembers it for the rest of the call.
  • Record in the voice call. Only there do plan edits wait for a yes. In the text chat the door is changed at once.
  • Ask for the saving if it isn't on screen. Navi doesn't mention the 34.7 m by itself. Either show the counter in the edit, or add “how much does that save?”.
  • Say “the Arena auditorium”. There are two Arenas (F0 seminar room, F1 auditorium); the F1 one gives the numbers above.
  • Trim Navi's replies in the edit. The real replies run 4–6 sentences; keep the lines above and speed up the rest of the text on screen.
